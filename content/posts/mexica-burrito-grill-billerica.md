I called the building before I could read the sign. Coming up Boston Road in
Billerica: *I know that place, that's where the Dunks was, across from the
trampoline park.* It was. Dunkin' moved out, [Mexica Burrito
Grill](https://www.mymexica.com/) moved in, and whatever they did to the
inside, the outside still gives it away. You pull in knowing exactly what used
to be there.

Inside there is more room than I expected, and a lot of it is seating. **Then
the ground beef in those tacos turned out to be the most authentic taco meat I
have had here.**

## Down the line

Ordering has the Chipotle rhythm to it: your plate starts at one end of the
counter and works its way down while you point at what goes on it. It is a
format that gets a lot of grief, and it is the right call here. Nobody has to
know the menu's name for anything, you can see what you are getting, and the
line keeps moving.

## The tacos

Flour tortillas, ground beef. The beef is what makes it. **The seasoning is
spot on, an actual ground beef blend, and it has been a long time since I was
served one** rather than meat that was browned and salted. The first bite was
good and it still needed a little extra, and what it needed was heat.

So I went back up to the counter for hot sauce. It is one of the things you
are supposed to pay for. The guy working the counter caught my eye, waved me
off, handed it over and never said a word about it. **It was a verde with
barely any heat to it, and with that on there the taco was perfect.**

That is what I have been missing out here. I do not need it hot, and I do not
need a novelty sauce with a warning on the label. I want a mild green salsa
that belongs on what it came with.

The one thing I went looking for and did not find was a soft corn tortilla.
Flour or a hard shell as far as I could see, so next time I am ordering the
hard shell to get corn into it and seeing where that lands. It did not take
anything away from the meal — I just wanted the option.

My wife had a burrito with steak and I got a bite of it. The steak is right
there with the ground beef, seasoned by the same hand, no drop-off between
them. That is two things off the menu now and both of them were good.

## The verdict

Mexica is in the weekly rotation. The tacos are good and the drive is short,
which is all it takes to earn the spot. Next time is the hard shell for the
corn, with the verde picked up on the way to the table instead of after the
first bite.

Bravo, Mexica. This is the first of these I have written down — I eat a lot of
tacos and this blog has been nothing but code, so there is a taco in the header
now and more of these coming.

## Where

**Mexica Burrito Grill**, 647 Boston Road, Billerica, MA 01821. Monday to
Saturday 11am–10pm, Sunday 11am–9pm at the time of writing. There is a second
location in Tewksbury at 759 Main Street, which I have not been to yet. Both
take [orders online](https://www.mymexica.com/).
